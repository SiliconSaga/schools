# Proposal Modules

> **Archived, with corrections.** These modules were written in April 2026, before the budget passed, and are kept as written apart from source and wording corrections made in October 2026. They were drafted quickly, as starting points for volunteers to pick up and test, not as finished plans.

Each module addresses a specific cost-avoidance opportunity or structural reform.
They work together -- savings from some modules fund the proposals in others.
