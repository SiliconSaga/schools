# The Ledger

A public record of the outsourcing of paraprofessionals in West Orange Public Schools in 2026: what the Board decided, what the district told families, which records are being requested, and what comes back.

It is kept by one parent, Rasmus Praestholm, on his own behalf. It does not speak for any PTA, parent group, or union, and it is not affiliated with the district.

## The short version

- **April 29, 2026.** The Superintendent told families that outsourcing paraprofessional services would save $3 million a year and that "our students will continue to see the same familiar faces they rely on." ([letter, p. 3](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1777488819/woboeorg/biirh6zs6kbwvcamc67c/WestOrangeCommunityBudgetDetails4292026English.pdf))
- **June 16.** The Board approved an agreement with the teachers' union permitting the outsourcing, and at the same meeting rejected the proposals it had received under its own request for proposals for the work. ([minutes, pp. 12 and 25](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1784732860/woboeorg/jryunce7m2qtccixad76/June162026MINUTES.pdf))
- **July 21.** The Board approved an "EduStaff Pricing Schedule Addendum-Exhibit B for an estimated amount of $12,000,000." ([minutes, p. 13](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1787848637/woboeorg/aecbvgbymysfx09km6um/July212026-Minutes.pdf))
- **September 28.** The district reported at the Board meeting that 176 of 244 needed paraprofessional positions were filled: 47 returning aides, 13 in grant-funded or pension positions, and 116 new. It said 24 positions were vacant and 44 hires were in process, and that it is working with EduStaff to bring staff on. ([recording, from 53:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QcmkuVBK-Nc&t=3192s))
- **In writing.** As far as I can find, the district has not published these numbers, the staffing agreement, or what has been billed for the work.

The full sequence is on the [timeline](/ledger/timeline). The district's statements are set beside the records on [What the District Said](/ledger/statements).

## How to read this

Every entry is labelled with what it rests on.

- <span class="label label-blue">Record</span> A district or vendor document, linked, with the page number.
- <span class="label label-purple">Statement</span> Something an official said in a letter or at a public meeting, quoted, with the date.
- <span class="label label-yellow">Press</span> Something a named news outlet reported, linked.

Accounts from parents and staff are not published here. They are real and they matter, but a second-hand account is not a record, and publishing one could expose the person who gave it. The requests above ask for the documents that would confirm or correct what people are describing.

## Corrections and replies

If anything here is wrong, write to **schools@frontstate.org**. Corrections are made with a dated note and the earlier wording stays visible. Any response from the district will be published in full.
