# Records Requests

New Jersey's Open Public Records Act (OPRA) lets anyone ask a public agency for its records. These are the requests to the West Orange Board of Education made for this ledger, with their status. "Filed" means hand-delivered to the district; "Response due" is the applicable deadline, which a written extension from the district can move.

{% include opra-table.html %}

## How the deadlines work

- The district has seven business days after it receives a request to grant or deny it. Contracts, bills, and vouchers less than two years old are ordinarily due immediately. The law allows longer in a few defined cases, such as requests made for a commercial purpose, and a custodian may ask in writing for more time when records are in storage or need review.
- If the district neither answers nor asks for more time, the law treats the silence as a denial.
- A denial can be challenged within 45 days, at the state Government Records Council or in Superior Court.

The law is N.J.S.A. 47:1A-1 and following, as amended in 2024. The Government Records Council posts the [full text](https://www.nj.gov/grc/laws/act/).

## What gets published

For each request: the text as filed, the date-stamped receipt, every response from the district in full, and the records themselves. The requester's phone number is removed; his name and address appear as filed, by his own choice. Student names and other personal details are not requested, and would be removed if they arrived. The names of individual employees, including paraprofessionals, are removed from any record that carries them; the counts and the rest of the record are kept.
