## Notes for you (not part of the request)

1. **The legal-proceeding certification.** Since the 2024 amendments a requester must certify whether the records are sought in connection with a legal proceeding and identify it. If you have a due process petition, a state complaint, or any other proceeding already filed about your own children's services, decide how to answer this truthfully before filing; that is a question for a lawyer or the Government Records Council, not for me.
2. **File under your name with accurate contact details.** An anonymous requester cannot challenge a denial, and a non-response to one is not a deemed denial.
3. **Use the district's form.** It is linked from https://www.woboe.org/departments/business-office/opra-request (dated June 2026; a copy is in `../sources/opra-form.pdf`). It is a scanned image I could not read. Fill in the form and write "see attached" in the records box, with the request text above as the attachment; a letter or email alone counts only if it carries everything the form asks for.
4. **Who the custodian probably is.** The district site still lists Tonya Flowers as Business Administrator/Board Secretary, but the July 21 minutes authorize Trenae Lambkin to award contracts from May 25, 2026, and a July news report has her as acting business administrator and board secretary. Ask at the counter who the custodian of records is and write the name down.
5. **Why item 2 matters.** The Board rejected the RFP proposals on June 16 and then approved a pricing addendum on July 21. No document I found states what procurement basis the paraprofessional work was awarded on. That is my reading of the minutes, not a finding; the records in items 1, 2, and 4 will show it either way.
6. **Why no emails.** A request for correspondence must name job titles or accounts, a subject, and a time period, and it invites extensions. Keeping this to contracts and bills keeps it in the fast lane.
7. **Held back for a follow-up once the contract is in hand:** any reports the contract requires the vendor to deliver (request them by the name the contract uses), the vendor's insurance and background-check certifications, and the Sunbelt Staffing substitute agreement approved August 24.

## Hand delivery, then email

- Bring two printed copies of each request with its completed form. Ask for one to be date-stamped "received" and handed back, or for a signed and dated receipt. Photograph it before you leave.
- Send the same text by email from the lobby to the address the form or the staff give you, and note the time. The stamped copy and the sent email are the two receipts the ledger publishes, with your address and phone redacted.
- Contracts and bills are immediate-access records, so it is reasonable to ask at the counter whether the EduStaff agreement can be handed over or emailed that day. Expect to be told it will follow.
- **The clock:** seven business days after the day of receipt. Received Thursday, October 1, that is Monday, October 12 if the district office is open on Columbus Day, and Tuesday, October 13 if it is closed. Check the district calendar and log whichever applies. Silence past that date is a deemed denial, and a denial can be challenged within 45 days at the Government Records Council or in Superior Court.
