## Notes for you (not part of the request)

1. **The legal-proceeding certification.** Since the 2024 amendments a requester must certify whether the records are sought in connection with a legal proceeding and identify it. The request text certifies that they are not; the owner confirmed on October 13, 2026 that there is no related proceeding. If one is filed later, that line has to change before the next request goes in.
2. **File under your name with accurate contact details.** An anonymous requester cannot challenge a denial, and a non-response to one is not a deemed denial.
3. **Why item 2 matters.** The Board rejected the RFP proposals on June 16 and then approved a pricing addendum on July 21. No document I found states what procurement basis the paraprofessional work was awarded on. That is my reading of the minutes, not a finding; the records in items 1, 2, and 4 will show it either way.
4. **Why no emails.** A request for correspondence must name job titles or accounts, a subject, and a time period, and it invites extensions. Keeping this to contracts and bills keeps it in the fast lane.
5. **Held back for a follow-up once the contract is in hand:** any reports the contract requires the vendor to deliver (request them by the name the contract uses), the vendor's insurance and background-check certifications, and the Sunbelt Staffing substitute agreement approved August 24 (now named in OPRA-003 item 3).
6. **Contracts and bills are immediate-access records**, so it is reasonable to ask at the counter whether the EduStaff agreement can be handed over or emailed that day. Expect to be told it will follow.
