# OPRA-002 — Paraprofessional coverage records: notes

The request text lives in one place, `_includes/opra/002-coverage.md`. The ledger page `ledger/opra/002-coverage.md` includes it, and `tools/opra-print.py` renders it with the requester's details for filing. Edit the include, not this file, to change what is asked for. Filing steps and the deadline arithmetic are in `filing.md`. Not legal advice.

## Notes for you (not part of the request)

1. **Ask for documents, not numbers.** OPRA does not make the district compute a fill rate. Each item names a document or an export that either exists or does not; a "no responsive records" answer to item 1 or 2 is itself a finding worth logging.
2. **Item 4 puts the spoken numbers on paper.** The September 28 recording has the Director of Human Resources reading out 244 positions needed, 176 working (13 grant-funded or pension, 47 returning, 116 new), 44 onboarding, and 24 vacant. She was reading from something; this item asks for it.
3. **Item 2 is the one most likely to be contested.** Paff held that pulling stored fields is not creating a new record, but the 2024 amendments added a statutory no-research clause and no court has yet said how the two fit together. Expect pushback and decide in advance whether you would take it to the Government Records Council.
4. **Vendor-held records are uncertain ground.** Records the vendor sent the District, or that the contract obliges it to send, are reachable. The vendor's internal timesheets may not be. OPRA-001 returns the contract, which tells you which reports to ask for by name next time.
5. **Refile monthly.** The same request with a new period gives the ledger a time series built from the district's own data.
6. **Not included: the field-trip question.** A request for any written directive on field-trip participation and aide staffing would need named job titles, a subject, and a date range. The parent email sent to the Director of Special Services on September 30 may answer it without a request; a fourth request can follow once that reply or its absence is known.
7. **File this separately from OPRA-001**, the same day, so a delay on one does not hold the other.
