# OPRA-002 — Paraprofessional coverage records (DRAFT)

Status: draft for review, not filed. Bracketed items are yours to fill in. Not legal advice.

**To:** Custodian of Records, West Orange Board of Education, Business Office, 179 Eagle Rock Avenue, West Orange, NJ 07052

**Subject:** OPRA request — paraprofessional coverage records, September 1, 2026 to date

This is a request for government records under the Open Public Records Act, N.J.S.A. 47:1A-1 et seq., and the common law right of access.

**Requester:** [full name], [street address], [email], [phone]

**Certifications:** This request is not made for a commercial purpose. The records are not sought in connection with a legal proceeding. [Confirm this is true for you before filing.]

**Format:** Electronic copies by email. Where a record exists as a spreadsheet or a system export, please send it in that form.

**Period:** September 1, 2026 through the date of this request.

**Records requested:**

1. Every report delivered to the District during the period by EduStaff, or by any other vendor providing paraprofessional staffing, concerning fill rates, absences, vacancies, or unfilled paraprofessional assignments.
2. A report or export from the absence-management or scheduling system used for paraprofessional assignments, whether the District operates it or the vendor operates it on the District's behalf, showing for each school day in the period, by school building, the number of paraprofessional assignments, the number filled, and the number unfilled. If no report with exactly those fields exists, please send the closest standard report that system produces.
3. The most recent existing document(s) showing paraprofessional positions or assignments by school for 2026-27 and which of them are vacant, such as a staffing allocation list or vacancy list.
4. Any presentation, slides, or written report on paraprofessional staffing delivered at the September 28, 2026 Board meeting.
5. The classroom aide or paraprofessional job description(s) in effect for 2026-27, and the county office of education's approval of them under N.J.A.C. 6A:14-4.1(e).

**Notes for the custodian:**

- I am asking for existing records and stored data, not for research or new analysis. Item 2 asks for fields already held in a database; see Paff v. Galloway Township, 229 N.J. 340 (2017).
- I am not seeking student names or any personally identifiable student information, and I do not need the names of individual paraprofessionals. Please redact those and note the basis for each redaction. Counts by building and date are what matter.
- If any item is denied in whole or in part, please state the specific legal basis for each denial, as N.J.S.A. 47:1A-5(g) requires.
- If you expect to assess a special service charge, please send an itemized estimate before doing the work.

## Notes for you (not part of the request)

1. **Ask for documents, not numbers.** OPRA does not make the district compute a fill rate. Each item above names a document or an export that either exists or does not; a "no responsive records" answer to item 1 or 2 is itself a finding worth logging.
2. **Item 4 puts the spoken numbers on paper.** The September 28 recording has the Director of Human Resources reading out 244 positions needed, 176 working (13 grant-funded or pension, 47 returning, 116 new), 44 onboarding, and 24 vacant. She was reading from something; this item asks for it.
3. **Item 2 is the one most likely to be contested.** Paff held that pulling stored fields is not creating a new record, but the 2024 amendments added a statutory no-research clause and no court has yet said how the two fit together. Expect pushback and decide in advance whether you would take it to the Government Records Council.
4. **Vendor-held records are uncertain ground.** Records the vendor sent the District, or that the contract obliges it to send, are reachable. The vendor's internal timesheets may not be. OPRA-001 returns the contract, which tells you which reports to ask for by name next time.
5. **Refile monthly.** The same request with a new period gives the ledger a time series built from the district's own data.
6. **Not included: the field-trip question.** A request for any written directive on field-trip participation and aide staffing would need named job titles, a subject, and a date range. The parent email sent to the Director of Special Services on September 30 may answer it without a request; hold OPRA-003 until that reply or its absence is known.
7. **File this separately from OPRA-001**, the same day, so a delay on one does not hold the other. The hand-delivery steps and the deadline arithmetic are in the OPRA-001 notes.
